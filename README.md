## Dayflow - Human Resource Management System

### What We're Building
Dayflow simplifies everyday HR tasks by bringing everything into one place. It helps teams stay on top of onboarding, employee records, daily attendance tracking, leave requests, and payroll updates without all the usual administrative back-and-forth.

### System Roles
- **Admin / HR Officer:** Has full access to manage employee profiles, track attendance records across teams, approve or reject leave requests, and handle salary structures.
- **Employee:** Has a personal portal to view their own profile, log daily check-ins and check-outs, apply for time off, and review their salary details.
