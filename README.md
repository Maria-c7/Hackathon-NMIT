## Dayflow - Human Resource Management System

### What We're Building
Dayflow simplifies everyday HR tasks by bringing everything into one place. It helps teams stay on top of onboarding, employee records, daily attendance tracking, leave requests, and payroll updates without all the usual administrative back-and-forth.

### System Roles
- **Admin / HR Officer:** Has full access to manage employee profiles, track attendance records across teams, approve or reject leave requests, and handle salary structures.
- **Employee:** Has a personal portal to view their own profile, log daily check-ins and check-outs, apply for time off, and review their salary details.
### Key System Features

#### 1. Authentication & Role-Based Access
- Secure Sign Up & Sign In using Employee ID, email, and password.
- Dual role access: **Admin / HR Officer** and **Employee**.

#### 2. Employee Self-Service Portal
- **Profile Management:** View personal details, job role, profile picture, and salary structure.
- **Attendance Marking:** Daily check-in and check-out tracking with real-time status updates (Present, Absent, Half-day, Leave).
- **Leave Requests:** Apply for Paid, Sick, or Unpaid leave with custom date ranges and remarks.
- **Payroll View:** Read-only access to personal salary details.

#### 3. Admin & HR Dashboard
- **Employee Directory:** Overview of all registered staff and profile management.
- **Attendance Oversight:** Monitor daily/weekly attendance records across the organization.
- **Leave Approvals:** Review pending requests and approve or reject with comments.
- **Payroll Controls:** Manage employee salary structures and ensure payment accuracy.
